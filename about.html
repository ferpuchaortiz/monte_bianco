Our Story

Monte Bianco was founded by a small team of gelato enthusiasts who fell in love with the flavours and textures of traditional Italian ice cream while travelling through the Alps. Inspired by mountain cafés and family‑run gelaterias, they brought the idea back to the Gold Coast and began crafting small‑batch ice cream using slow‑churn methods and fresh cream.

Mission

Our mission is to create ice cream that feels like a moment of escape: honest ingredients, balanced sweetness, and flavours that tell a story. Every scoop is made in‑house in small batches to keep quality and freshness at the centre of what we do.

Vision

We aim to become a local landmark for ice‑cream lovers, known for thoughtful flavour combinations, warm service, and a welcoming space where friends and families gather.

Values

  • Using natural, carefully sourced ingredients
  • Crafting flavours with creativity and care
  • Reducing waste and respecting the environment
  • Offering friendly, attentive service to every guest
